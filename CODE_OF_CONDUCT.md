# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Jobgoblin to be a welcoming project for everyone who contributes, whatever their background, experience, or circumstances. People are often looking for work when they use this project, and we treat that with care.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community:

- Being respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Giving and accepting feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Acknowledging that everyone starts somewhere, and helping newcomers.
- Being honest about what you know and what you do not.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any channel.
- Discrimination based on who someone is.
- Publishing someone else's personal information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and commits) and when you represent the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer: [ADD CONTACT BEFORE PUBLISHING]. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer reviews each one, and responds in a way that fits the situation, from a private reminder to removing someone from the project. Everyone involved in a report is treated with respect and confidentiality.
